# Research Scientist - LLM

## About the role
Research-driven role for ML researchers pushing real-time AI. Focus on advancing
model capabilities for human-like voice agents in complex, real-world environments.
Explore new approaches across LLMs and audio models, design novel evaluation methods,
prototype systems improving reasoning, latency, and conversational quality. Bridges
cutting-edge research with real-world deployment.

## Key responsibilities
- Research & experimentation across LLMs and audio models to improve reasoning,
  latency, and conversational quality in real-time systems
- Model training: rapidly build and iterate on models and pipelines, turning research
  ideas into working prototypes
- Evaluation & benchmarking: design novel evaluation frameworks, datasets, and
  metrics for complex, real-world voice tasks
- Bridge research to production: collaborate with engineering to translate research
  insights into deployable systems
- Human feedback loops: incorporate human evaluation into model improvement

## Required
- Strong ML research background (LLM pre-training/post-training, transcription model
  training, TTS, or multimodal systems)
- Deep technical foundation: PyTorch, model architectures, the math behind modern ML
- Master's degree in CS/ML/AI required, PhD preferred

## Nice to have
- Publications at top-tier venues (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, Interspeech)
- Experimental mindset, comfort with open-ended problems
- Ability to bridge theory and practice — translating research into working systems
- Startup-ready, high ownership, clear communicator
